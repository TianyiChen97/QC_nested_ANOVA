
## Scale decision
Primary analysis: ln(mean ISI) (all figures, tests, post-hoc). Raw mean ISI (ms) is the sensitivity check in `<tp>/sensitivity_raw/`.
Known difference: at mid DIV the well(run) effect is significant on ln (F p~3e-5) but not on raw ms (F p=0.060, perm p=0.074). ln residuals are heavy-tailed (excess kurtosis ~14-16) because of electrodes with very short mean ISI.
